# Roadmap

Version 0.4 connects the original playable foundation with practice skills, six projects, fatigue/sleep, finite survivor requests and trust, seeded world events, surface mining, gardens, stockpiles, companion work, faction diplomacy, staged battles, appearance and pets. The journal explains these systems. Feature depth and balance still need sustained development.

## Next improvements

- Deeper building plans, interactive furniture and dedicated rooms; richer procedural towns and landmarks.
- Vehicle parts and differentiated repairs, trunk storage, seats, companion passengers, and better driving feel. Current field repairs restore overall condition.
- Companion following and travel beyond the loaded window, full NPC inventories, varied requests, meaningful personalities, richer schedules, camps and relationships. Current survivors have short local routes, daily bandage stock and saved trust.
- Richer item actions and tool uses. Six manuals support one-time study; several other utility objects remain collectible ingredients rather than independent simulations.
- More crop varieties, farming depth, irrigation, cooking depth, refrigeration, food spoilage, weather exposure and temperature. The current garden grows one crop through moist time.
- Power, water networks, generators, plumbing, and outages.
- Wear and repair of weapons, richer wounds, more skill branches, professions and character creation.
- Construction expansion, doors/windows, player shelter interiors and a wider recipe catalogue.
- More floor layouts, visibility and interaction between floors.
- Deeper world events with lasting regional changes, survivor memories that affect more choices, and broader projects with multiple solutions.
- Richer faction strategy, lasting territorial changes, varied alliances, battlefield navigation and a more complete settlement economy.
- Underground resource layers, distinct geology and more mining tools; current resources are seeded surface deposits.
- More cosmetic choices, richer pet behaviors, safe travel with companions/pets and interaction across floors.
- Richer animation and audio, configurable key bindings, accessibility settings and performance measurements on more hardware.
- Longer natural playtesting, world-journal pruning or migration, and more browser/device validation.

## Larger work

Independent multiplayer regions, upper floors, PvP and relay traversal, full NPC communities, a handcrafted regional map, mod loading and extensive simulation need separate architecture and substantial implementation. There is no claim of full Project Zomboid feature or item parity. No release date is promised for unfinished work.

## Production quality target

AAA quality is a longer production target, not a label for this early playable release. Reaching it requires a coherent survival loop over many hours, readable interactions, reliable navigation and saves, and content whose systems remain useful beyond their first encounter.

- Playtest long runs for scavenging scarcity, shelter recovery, progression pacing, combat fairness and meaningful survivor choices.
- Expand interiors, storage and construction so a base supports organizing supplies, repairing gear and preparing for travel.
- Improve zombie and human navigation, group behavior, travel continuity and readable reactions to danger.
- Add deeper item condition, food and water handling, weather exposure, injury treatment and vehicle systems with clear requirements and consequences.
- Provide configurable controls, accessible visual/audio feedback, comfortable camera options and consistent menus across supported displays.
- Measure frame time, save reliability, generation seams and memory over long trips on several browsers and hardware classes.
- Establish a cohesive art direction, richer animation and sound, onboarding, useful help and a sustained regression/release process.

## Current multiplayer and AI boundaries

Local 20-player hosting, public listings, private invites and bounded neural-assisted AI play are implemented. Remaining work includes long public-internet sessions, guest-targeting parity, independent far-away regions, PvP, anti-cheat hardening, automatic NAT traversal and an operated discovery service. The small local network is validated as an implementation; superior gameplay performance needs comparative playtesting.
