# Roadmap

Version 0.3 establishes an original playable foundation. Feature depth and balance still need sustained development.

## Next improvements

- More distinctive building plans, rooms and furniture; richer procedural towns and landmarks.
- Vehicle repair, parts, trunk storage, seats, companion passengers, and better driving feel.
- Companion following and travel beyond the loaded window, NPC inventories, finite trade stock, schedules, camps and relationships.
- Richer item actions and tool uses. Books and several utility objects are collectible ingredients today, not independent simulations.
- Farming, cooking depth, refrigeration, food spoilage, weather exposure and temperature.
- Power, water networks, generators, plumbing, and outages.
- Wear and repair of weapons, richer wounds, skills, professions and character creation.
- Construction expansion, doors/windows, player shelter interiors and a wider recipe catalogue.
- More floor layouts, visibility and interaction between floors.
- Richer animation and audio, accessibility settings and performance measurements on more hardware.
- Longer natural playtesting, world-journal pruning or migration, and more browser/device validation.

## Larger work

Multiplayer, full NPC communities, a handcrafted regional map, mod loading and extensive simulation need separate architecture and substantial implementation. There is no claim of full Project Zomboid feature or item parity. No release date is promised for unfinished work.
